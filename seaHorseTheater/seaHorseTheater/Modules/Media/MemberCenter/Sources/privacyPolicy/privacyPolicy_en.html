Haima Theater App Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Thank you for using our application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information. Please read it carefully before using the app.

2. Information We Collect

Our app does not include account registration or login, and therefore does not collect any personally identifiable information such as name, phone number, or email address.

We may collect the following information for functional purposes:

  • Device information (device type, OS version, device identifier, for performance analysis only)
  • Usage data (e.g., page views, tap actions, for product improvement)
  • Subscription data (provided by Apple, to determine if you are a premium user)
  • Sharing behavior analytics (whether you triggered a "share app" action; no contact or content info is collected)

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide you with complete subscription services (e.g., ad removal, unlocking premium features)
  • To analyze user behavior and improve product experience
  • To determine whether to display subscription-related UI or prompts
  • To optimize sharing flows (not the content or recipient of the share)

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties except in the following situations:

  • To comply with laws or government requests
  • Apple processes your purchases and payments through its subscription system

We do not access your payment information. All transactions are handled through the Apple App Store.

5. Third-Party Services

This app may use the following third-party services:

  • Apple In-App Purchase (IAP) — Processes your subscription transactions
  • Apple StoreKit / RevenueCat (if used) — Retrieves subscription status and manages subscriptions
  • Firebase / Apple Analytics (if enabled) — For product analysis and crash tracking
  • System sharing components — Users select platforms to share app links; we do not access or store your contacts or shared content

6. Data Storage & Security

We do not upload data to our own servers. All data is either stored locally or securely handled by Apple.

We apply reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

7. Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. If we learn we have collected such data, we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

You can always restrict the app’s access to data (e.g., notifications, sharing) via your system settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you through in-app alerts or changelogs.
